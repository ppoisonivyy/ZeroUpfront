# Where ZeroUpfront fits

A hospital billing team can use the synthetic workspace to trace a coverage issue from a queue row to a physical page, correct the packet, and inspect the exact revision it hands off. These comparisons concern the documented workflows. There has been no head-to-head study of time saved, denial rates or payer acceptance.

| Existing workflow | What it offers | How the demo compares |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bangkok Hospital Pattaya direct billing | The hospital describes contacting insurers for a guarantee of payment and requesting additional authorization if the cost or treatment changes. Responsibility and deposits depend on the guarantee. | The pre-arrival desk preserves evidence, staff review and written terms. The demo insurer inbox exercises the handoff with a fictional payer. A pilot needs a participating hospital and payer to confirm their form edition, channel and decision rules. [Hospital insurance process](https://www.bangkokhospital.com/en/pattaya/visit/insurance-bph) |
| Availity claim and status services | Health care providers can submit transactions and obtain claim status across participating payers. | ZeroUpfront focuses on what supports each request and which revision was sent. The demo does not provide Availity’s payer connectivity or live adjudication. [Availity claim and status](https://www.availity.com/claim-and-claim-status/) |
| Waystar Claim Manager | Waystar describes applying edits earlier in the lifecycle and managing claims through a clearinghouse. | ZeroUpfront lets a reviewer open the cited page, see a correction and inspect earlier originals. Its synthetic checks demonstrate supported cases. Clearinghouse-scale rule coverage and operational outcomes have not been validated. [Waystar Claim Manager](https://www.waystar.com/our-platform/claim-management/claim-manager/) |
| FX reference and transfer quotes | Frankfurter publishes dated central-bank references. Wise distinguishes indicative quotes from authenticated quotes used in a transfer. | The funding desk uses a real dated reference, simulated bank offers and separate beneficiary credit advice. A hospital gets a trace from guarantee to bank advice. A contracted provider would have to book and settle an actual transfer. [Frankfurter](https://frankfurter.dev/) · [Wise quotes](https://docs.wise.com/guides/product/send-money/quotes) |

The payer-form evaluation held out new synthetic case values and combinations on familiar form editions. It did not hold out an entire payer. A future comparison should use unseen payer rulebooks, authorized examples and independent staff adjudication, then measure missed gaps, false holds, time to reviewed submission and queries returned after submission.
