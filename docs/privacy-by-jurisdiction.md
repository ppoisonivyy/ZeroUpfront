# Protecting hospital claims across jurisdictions

Use one technical baseline and a **versioned privacy decision for each hospital and data flow**. A claim can involve more than one jurisdiction. A US form, patient nationality or cloud-region label alone cannot settle which laws apply. Record who determines the purpose, where the parties operate, whose information is involved, who can access it and what the recipient will do. Apply all relevant obligations. If retention, disclosure or location requirements conflict, the hospital's privacy officer and local counsel should resolve the specific flow before enabling it.

## What the release can demonstrate

The release supports a dedicated database for each hospital, individual staff accounts, session revocation, original exhibits linked to immutable claim revisions, and a record of review and settlement actions. Coordinators share their hospital queue. Finance accounts have a read-only list of assigned discharge balances and cannot access case documents. The operator controls external AI processing separately through the OCR and second-read flags. Digital intake works with both off. The example and benchmark data are synthetic. [Deployment and feature settings](deployment.md) · [Staff account controls](../app/staff_access.py) · [Database rehearsal](discharge-scale-benchmark.md)

A real deployment still needs evidence of who may see each case, where the database and backups run, which third parties can process identifiable records, how patients exercise rights, when records expire and who handles incidents. The claim workflow records staff decisions. A complete access log should also account for reads, previews, downloads, privileged support and vendor access. Privacy notices, jurisdiction selection, patient rights administration, retention enforcement and breach reporting require a hospital process or additional implementation.

## Overseas processing, replicas and optional OpenAI calls

- **Thailand.** The PDPC notifications under §§28 and 29 took effect on March 24, 2024. Identify the foreign recipient and onward access. Record the applicable route, such as adequate protection or a specific §28 exception, certified intra-group policy where applicable, or qualifying §29 safeguards with enforceable rights and remedies. Assess the notifications’ definition and exclusions against the service actually used. A provider that reads a PDF for OCR or inference processes its contents. A Thai database region alone leaves that disclosure unresolved. The Thai-language Act and notifications govern. [Act §§28–29](https://www.dit.go.th/media/csfpmuih/20260910153851_8894.pdf) · [§28 Royal Gazette notification](https://ratchakitcha.soc.go.th/documents/14915.pdf) · [§29 Royal Gazette notification](https://ratchakitcha.soc.go.th/documents/14913.pdf)
- **EU/EEA.** Map each applicable third-country transfer under GDPR Chapter V, including onward transfers. Record adequacy, an applicable safeguard such as the relevant standard contractual clauses, or a narrowly applicable derogation. Where relying on safeguards, assess whether they work in the destination and add effective supplementary measures where needed. The hospital should assess a data protection impact assessment for likely high-risk processing, including large-scale processing of sensitive data, and DPO requirements. [GDPR Articles 35, 37 and 44–49](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng/) · [Commission transfer guidance](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/rules-international-data-transfers_en)
- **US.** HHS permits covered entities and business associates to use overseas cloud providers subject to applicable HIPAA requirements, including the BAA and risk analysis. Geographic, legal and access risks should inform the hospital’s provider selection. Check additional state, payer and contract restrictions, including where backups and privileged support reside. [HHS cloud guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/health-information-technology/cloud-computing/index.html)
- **OpenAI.** The release’s optional second read sends the available original PDFs and selected case metadata. OCR sends selected source pages. Both set `store=False`. The provider distinguishes response storage from abuse-monitoring logs, which can retain customer content for up to 30 days by default, with stated exceptions. Check the approved organization, project, model, endpoint, filename/content handling, processing region, retention settings, subprocessors and applicable agreements for the exact feature. For US PHI, verify a signed BAA and covered API configuration. OpenAI lists API with Modified Retention as eligible. Hackathon credits establish a billing allowance. Keep real records out of an unapproved project. [OCR code](../app/document_text.py) · [Second-read code](../app/payer_ai_reader.py) · [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data) · [OpenAI BAA and eligible products](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001069-hipaa-eligible-products-and-functionality)

## Onboarding another hospital

A hospital in Bangkok treating a traveler with a US payer is a useful example. Map the hospital-to-payer disclosure, the service operator’s access, and the optional hospital-to-model-vendor disclosure individually. Check whether a US entity is engaging ZeroUpfront as a business associate. The payer form identifies the administrative workflow. The hospital and payer contracts, applicable laws and actual processing establish who may receive which documents.
