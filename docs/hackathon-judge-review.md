## What changed on official forms

On main, all 16 fictional packets using four official form editions stayed on hold, including both aligned AIA controls. The software named the intended issue in **1 of 12** targeted variations. In one Thai case OCR read THB 92,750 from page 2, but the checker missed the THB 5,250 difference from the THB 87,500 bill. [AIA baseline](thai-payer-forms.md)

PR #5 reports **12 of 12** on those same regression cases. On a separate frozen challenge, the first run identified **10 of 11** intended outcomes with the correct page. The team fixed the payee comparison and the cents citation. The same cases then reached **11 of 11**. We independently rescored the saved observations and exercised the integrated reader on synthetic test PDFs. Changing a form amount by one cent produced a discrepancy citing the form and bill. An aligned amount still allowed signature review to remain open. In the recorded follow-up, 22 of 45 aligned fields had explicit corroboration, nine were referred for manual review and 14 had no targeted result. These numbers support a field-checking claim on the tested editions. [Frozen challenge](https://github.com/aowngong/ZeroUpfront/blob/3949bf4/docs/payer-holdout.md) · [Follow-up audit](https://github.com/aowngong/ZeroUpfront/blob/3949bf4/docs/payer-model-fidelity.md)
