## 2. Definitions and evidence of membership
NQ-2.1 An admission means continuous overnight inpatient care under a single encounter reference. Two facility statements associated with one encounter may be interim and cumulative versions of the same charges.
NQ-2.2 Planned overseas care means travel arranged for a scheduled procedure. An unplanned injury during travel remains an accident admission even when the operating theatre is booked for the next morning.
NQ-2.3 Match the membership reference, date of service and facility encounter before attaching evidence. A transliterated name alone is insufficient. Record a mismatch for staff to resolve before releasing a draft to the assistance desk.
## 3. Overseas admissions and notification
NQ-3.1 For a planned foreign admission, attach the treatment plan, named provider, expected dates and itemized local-currency provider estimate before admission. A signed pre-check records packet readiness only.
NQ-3.2 For an unplanned overseas accident, the base notice window is 48 hours from admission. A rider on the member schedule may extend this window. Attach the time-stamped notice or assistance-desk receipt.
NQ-3.3 A scheduling email is evidence of the theatre booking only. If the member traveled before the accident, determine the overseas pathway from the event timeline and the admission record.
## 4. Costs, currencies and member share
NQ-4.1 The hospital submits in local currency. For the training certificate use the rate recorded on the assistance desk rate sheet for the admission date. Preserve both currencies, the rate direction and the rate source in the draft.
NQ-4.2 Apply the room allowance separately per covered overnight stay. The G2 cap is USD 220 each night. Room excess is the positive difference between actual room charge and the converted room allowance. Unused allowance does not transfer between nights.
NQ-4.3 Dedicated implants require a supplier estimate identifying the device, quantity and unit price. An all-inclusive theatre estimate does not replace the missing supplier schedule. Keep the line pending while staff obtain it.
## 5. Exclusions and documentation exceptions
NQ-5.1 A procedure solely for cosmetic appearance is excluded from the standard schedule. Restoration following a documented accidental injury may be submitted for individual review with the injury report and surgeon's description of the functional objective.
NQ-5.2 Elective comfort items, visitor meals and room upgrades above the applicable cap are separately identified member expenses. Their appearance on an inpatient bill does not change their classification.
NQ-5.3 The reviewer records the reason and evidence for each questioned line. A question about one line does not convert every other service on the estimate into a denied expense.
## 6. Guarantee request and changes
NQ-6.1 The request must distinguish estimated facility charges, expected member share, items pending evidence and amount requested. Preserve submission currency and the signed revision identifier.
NQ-6.2 A previous guarantee applies to its specified procedure, dates and ceiling. If an admission estimate changes, send a revised itemization and identify the delta against the referenced guarantee. Keep the old letter with its original revision.
NQ-6.3 The insurer reply can approve, limit or query a request. A successful portal submission and a generated case number are acknowledgements of delivery. Record them separately from a written guarantee.
## 7. Endorsements and order of precedence
NQ-7.1 Rider M7 applies to G2 certificates listing M7 for accidental admissions in Thailand on or after 1 October 2026. It replaces the 48-hour notice deadline in NQ-3.2 with 72 hours. The itemized estimate and implant schedule still apply.
NQ-7.2 Rider M6 applied to earlier admissions and allowed 60 hours. It expires on 30 September 2026. A copy attached to the file after expiry does not extend it. For a new admission use the service date and active schedule.
NQ-7.3 An endorsement changes only the requirement it identifies. In particular M7 does not raise the room allowance, approve an implant or waive the requirement for a written insurer response.
## 8. Submission checklist and correspondence
NQ-8.1 Attach plan and edition, dated enrollment schedule, facility case ID, rate sheet where relevant, cost breakdown and member-share calculation. Record each missing exhibit by name on the cover letter.
NQ-8.2 Request a supplier schedule when the implant line lacks device-level detail. Include the provisional device amount as pending and preserve the submission revision. The assistance desk may query that line without closing the case.
NQ-8.3 Keep the original invoice, revised estimate and subsequent receipt as separate exhibits. An estimate is forward-looking. A statement records charges. Only an identified payment receipt establishes payment.
